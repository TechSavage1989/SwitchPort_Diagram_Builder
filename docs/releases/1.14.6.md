# Reversible workspace edits and complete switch pages

- Remove configurations without repetitive confirmations; Undo restores their
  position, selection and unsaved per-device edits.
- Clear all resets the workspace with Undo. Original configs, generated reports
  and application preferences remain untouched. Retained results are marked previous.
- Keep standalone-fitting switch/LAG/description blocks together on a fresh page,
  using the full standalone side-key layout when compact placement is insufficient.
- Preserve readable continuation for genuinely oversized details, shared ID colors,
  side VLAN keys, bottom symbols, optional titles and PNG/PDF output behavior.
- Undo history is memory-only until closing or opening another workspace.
