## Cleaner combined switch sheets and unified results

- Combined diagrams begin with each switch's own header; the generic workspace title bar is removed.
- Switch blocks use compact spacing and share one complete VLAN legend per sheet instead of repeating individual VLAN lists.
- Matching VLAN ID/name identities appear once in the legend and retain exactly the same colors across all switches. Conflicting or unnamed identities remain distinct and explicitly labeled.
- Readable A4 pagination, tagged/voice markers, native fills, LAG outlines and per-switch information are preserved. Preview PNGs and vector PDFs use the same scenes.
- Reorganized configuration/workspace, scoped diagram settings and build/results controls. One **Open chart** and **Open output folder** target the latest successful project, whether single or multiple switches.
- Failed/cancelled builds preserve the last successful output; source edits/reorder show an earlier-build notice rather than redirecting or disabling it. Per-configuration device/model settings remain independent.
- Individual-switch diagrams and existing naming, import, export and updater integrity behavior are unchanged.
